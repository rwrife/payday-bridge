Payday Bridge — plan the gap between paydays.

Pre-release concept description. The features below are planned and are not yet available in a shipping app.

Monthly totals do not tell you which paycheck can cover a bill before its due date. Payday Bridge is being built as a private, offline iPhone worksheet for people with uneven pay dates.

Enter your own dated cash snapshot, expected and confirmed income, and upcoming bills. Assign bills to pay envelopes and see explainable warnings when a funding date is late, an envelope is overcommitted, or an amount is still unknown. Expected income stays separate from confirmed coverage.

Compare changes in a what-if draft without changing your saved plan. Apply or discard deliberately. Keep monthly bill occurrences, paid marks and portable backups under your control. Versioned JSON backup and CSV export are planned.

No banking login, accounts, cloud sync, ads or trackers. No payments or automatic money movement. Figures are manual planning estimates, not verified bank balances or a guarantee that funds are available. Payday Bridge does not provide financial, tax or investment advice.

Designed for iPhone with accessible controls and clear uncertainty labels. Future dual-screen layouts are a design direction, not a current compatibility claim.
